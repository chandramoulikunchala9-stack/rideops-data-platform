## Impact
The run took 8,490 s against a median of about 980 s (`long_running_runs` query). Gold tables were
refreshed about 2 hours late. No data was lost.

## Root cause
A backlog of event files after a mobile-app outage arrived in one batch. The fixed 4-worker cluster
spilled shuffle data to disk.

## Resolution
Enabled autoscaling (1 to 3 workers on job clusters) and capped `maxFilesPerTrigger`, so a backlog
is worked off in several bounded micro-batches instead of one huge one.
