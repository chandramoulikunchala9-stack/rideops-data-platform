## Impact
SIMULATED cost data: daily spend reached about 5x the usual level on 2026-10-01 and 10-02 (the
`cost_anomaly` rule flagged both days, z-score > 3). The cause was an all-purpose dev cluster with 8
workers that ran for 48 hours.

## Root cause
The cluster had auto-termination disabled, and it was not created through the cluster policy.

## Resolution
1. Terminated the cluster; confirmed the next day's cost was back to baseline.
2. Enforced `policies/job_cluster_policy.json` (max workers, mandatory tags) and a workspace default
   of 30 minutes auto-termination for all-purpose compute.
